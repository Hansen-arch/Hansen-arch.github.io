## About

<div class="about" markdown>

<div class="about-text" markdown>

I have supervised excavation for boundary compliance in Riau, measured forest plots for an
above-ground biomass study on peatland, and digitized land cover from satellite imagery at
RECOFTC. I use QGIS, ArcGIS, Google Earth Engine, and Python with Pandas and Matplotlib.

I have also counted oil palms from UAV orthophotos
([Palm Tree Counting](projects/palm-tree-counting.md)), measured how often Sentinel-1 sees a
flood while it is rising ([Flood Coverage](projects/flood-coverage.md)), and built a quality
checker for GBIF species records ([BioSift](projects/biosift.md)). I mapped surface heat and
vegetation change in Pekanbaru ([Urban Heat Island](projects/urban-heat-island.md),
[Urban Green Space Change](projects/urban-green-space.md)).

I am looking for entry-level roles in GIS analysis, remote sensing, or environmental data
work, preferably connected to forest monitoring.
