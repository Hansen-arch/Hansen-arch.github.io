## About me

<div class="about" markdown>

<div class="about-text" markdown>

I am a forestry undergraduate at Universitas Riau, in my final year. My work has moved
between the field and the desk: supervising excavation for boundary compliance in Riau,
measuring forest plots for an above-ground biomass study on peatland, and digitizing land
cover from satellite imagery at RECOFTC. On the analysis side I use QGIS, ArcGIS, Google
Earth Engine, and Python with Pandas and Matplotlib.

Most of what is published here started as a question a map could not answer. I counted
24,966 oil palms from UAV orthophotos to see whether a detector can replace a hand count
([Palm Tree Counting](projects/palm-tree-counting.md)). I built
[Flood Coverage](projects/flood-coverage.md) to measure how rarely a satellite looks at a
flood while it is rising, from a year of acquisition metadata. I built
[BioSift](projects/biosift.md) because a species record is only as useful as its
coordinates. Closer to home, I mapped where Pekanbaru stores heat
([Urban Heat Island](projects/urban-heat-island.md)) and where it lost vegetation
([Urban Green Space Change](projects/urban-green-space.md)), because those two claims
are usually made in the same breath without being measured separately.

I am looking for entry-level roles in GIS analysis, remote sensing, or environmental data
work, preferably connected to forest monitoring.
